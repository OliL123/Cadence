Privacy Policy

Cadence and Chronicle · Effective 26 September 2026

Cadence and Chronicle are two versions of the same personal to-do app, published at olil123.github.io/Cadence and olil123.github.io/Chronicle. This page explains what information the app handles and why. There are no ads, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Your tasks and settings

Your tasks, groups, daily habits and preferences are stored on your device.

If you choose to turn on Sync, you create an account with an email address and password, and your task data is stored in the app's database (hosted by Supabase) so it can appear on your other devices. Only your signed-in account can read it. Sync is optional; the app works fully without it.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, the app asks for read-only access (calendar.readonly) and uses it only to:

  • list your calendars, so you can choose which ones to show; and
  • read your upcoming events, so they appear in the app's TODAY card.

The app cannot create, change or delete anything in your calendar. Your events are read directly from Google each time they're shown and are not stored on any server.

To stay connected without asking you to sign in to Google again, the app keeps a Google refresh token in its database, linked to your Sync account. It is used for one purpose only: obtaining short-lived access to read your calendar as described above. It is never shown to other users or sent anywhere else, and the app's database is configured so that no app client can read it.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show you your own calendar in the app. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by people.

Removing access

  • Disconnect in the app's calendar panel deletes the stored refresh token and asks Google to revoke it.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Signing out of Sync stops syncing. To have your Sync account and its data deleted, use the contact link below.

Other services the app contacts

  • Weather: the place you choose is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch a forecast.
  • Public holidays: the countries you choose are sent to Nager.Date (date.nager.at) to fetch holiday dates.
  • Fonts: text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

None of these receive your tasks, your calendar or your account details.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/OliL123/Cadence/issues.
